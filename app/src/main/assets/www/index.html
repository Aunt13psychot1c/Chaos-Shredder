How to Use

  1. Tap “Transformation Mode” and pick a style.
  2. Type, paste, upload a .txt, or tap “Load Sample” in the Input box.
  3. Watch your text transform in the Output box as you go.
  4. Check your counts in Text Statistics.
  5. Tap “Copy” or “Download Text” to keep your chaos.
  6. In Jabberwocky or Brain Rot mode, tap the 🎲 button for a brand-new version.
  7. In Cursed mode, slide the Curse level to choose how haunted it gets.

✦ Emojis pass through untouched.
